# User-Facing Capability Disclosures

## 1. Transparency Commitment

To protect consumer trust and comply with Indian consumer protection and digital financial norms, ARTHSAVORA provides clear, explicit capability disclosures regarding what the application does and does not do.

---

## 2. Core Mandatory Disclosures

### 2.1 Financial and Tax Advisory Boundaries
> **"ARTHSAVORA provides digital financial record-keeping, categorization, and computational assistance. It is NOT a registered Investment Adviser under SEBI regulations and does not provide investment, portfolio management, or certified tax advice. Financial summaries and tax estimations are for informational purposes only. Please consult a qualified Chartered Accountant or SEBI-registered professional for formal filings."**

### 2.2 Payment Execution Boundaries
> **"ARTHSAVORA does not hold customer funds, execute money transfers, or operate as a payment intermediary. Any invoice payment links or banking references facilitate external handoffs to your banking or UPI application."**

### 2.3 Tax Filing Disclaimer
> **"Tax calculations, GST summaries, and Section 115BAC computations are based on verified user entries and standard statutory formulas. ARTHSAVORA does not automatically submit returns to the GST Portal or Income Tax Department."**

### 2.4 Bank Connectivity & Account Aggregator
> **"Bank data retrieval is performed strictly upon explicit user consent via authorized Account Aggregator integration partners. ARTHSAVORA never stores or asks for your net banking passwords or ATM PINs."**
