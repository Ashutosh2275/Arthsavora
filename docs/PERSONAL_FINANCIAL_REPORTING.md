# ARTHSAVORA — PERSONAL FINANCIAL REPORTING

## 1. Overview & Use Cases

Personal financial reporting in ARTHSAVORA is tailored for salaried individuals, self-employed professionals, and independent earners. It consolidates personal bank accounts, savings, credit cards, investments, and personal living expenses into clean, actionable financial statements.

---

## 2. Key Personal Financial Statements

### 2.1 Personal Income & Expense Statement (Cash Flow Focus)
- **Income Streams:** Salary credits, interest income, dividend credits, freelance inflows, rental returns.
- **Living Expenses:** Housing, utilities, groceries, lifestyle, insurance premiums, education, and transport.
- **Personal Savings Rate:**
  $$\text{Savings Rate (\%)} = \left(\frac{\text{Net Inflow Surplus}}{\text{Total Income}}\right) \times 100$$

### 2.2 Personal Net Worth & Balance Sheet
- **Personal Assets:** Liquid bank balances, fixed deposits, mutual fund holdings, provident fund (EPF/PPF) balances.
- **Personal Liabilities:** Credit card balances, home loans, auto loans, personal loans.
- **Personal Net Worth:**
  $$\text{Net Worth} = \text{Total Personal Assets} - \text{Total Personal Liabilities}$$

---

## 3. Privacy & Cross-Workspace Boundary

1. **Strict Isolation:** Personal financial reports only aggregate accounts and transactions owned directly by the user in their personal workspace.
2. **Family Boundary:** Transactions in personal workspaces remain completely invisible to other members of a family workspace unless explicitly shared via `FamilyAccountShare`.
3. **Tax Document Index:** Organizes personal Form 16, 80C investment receipts (LIC, ELSS, PPF), and medical health insurance bills.
