# ARTHSAVORA — SMALL BUSINESS FINANCIAL REPORTING

## 1. Overview & Business Use Cases

Business financial reporting serves freelancers, consultants, boutique agencies, and service enterprises operating under Indian GST and Income Tax regulations.

---

## 2. Key Business Statements & Reports

### 2.1 Commercial Profit & Loss (P&L) Statement
- **Operating Revenue:** Client invoice collections, project milestones, professional retainer fees.
- **Cost of Sales / Direct Costs:** Subcontractor payouts, freelance contractor fees, third-party software licenses.
- **Operating Expenses:** Office lease, travel, hardware depreciation, telecommunications, professional insurance.
- **Operating Margin:**
  $$\text{Operating Margin (\%)} = \left(\frac{\text{Operating Profit}}{\text{Operating Revenue}}\right) \times 100$$

### 2.2 Accounts Receivable (AR) & Accounts Payable (AP) Aging
- Integrates with the invoicing and bills service to report overdue client invoices and pending vendor payables categorized by aging buckets:
  - 0–30 Days
  - 31–60 Days
  - 61–90 Days
  - 90+ Days

### 2.3 General Ledger & Account Statements
- Comprehensive debit/credit transaction history per ledger account with opening and closing balances for external accountant reconciliation.

### 2.4 Section 44ADA Presumptive Taxation Support
- For eligible professionals (software engineers, technical consultants, accountants, legal practitioners) with gross receipts $\le \text{₹75,00,000}$:
  $$\text{Presumptive Professional Income} = 50\% \times \text{Gross Professional Receipts}$$
- Provides instant comparison against actual documented expenditure.
